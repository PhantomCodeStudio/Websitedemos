## Universal Design Anti-Patterns

### ✗ Generic Startup Hero
- Centered headline + subheadline + button
- Stock photo background
- Generic AI gradient
- Zero personality
- Interchangeable with 1000 other sites
- **Why avoid:** Zero differentiation, boring, unmemorable

### ✗ Random Blue/Purple AI Gradients
- Neon blue to purple
- No connection to brand
- Overused to death
- Screams "generic SaaS"
- **Why avoid:** Visible genericism, loses credibility

### ✗ Glassmorphism Everywhere
- Frosted glass effect on every element
- No hierarchy of when to use
- Often reduces readability
- Outdated trend
- **Why avoid:** Dated, gimmicky, accessibility risk

### ✗ Weak Card Components
- No shadow or spacing definition
- Unclear click target
- Inconsistent sizing
- No hover feedback
- **Why avoid:** Poor UX, looks amateur

### ✗ Generic SaaS Feature Cards
- Icon + headline + one sentence
- Same layout repeated 6+ times
- No visual hierarchy
- Forgettable
- **Why avoid:** Unmemorable, low conversion

### ✗ Random 3D Blob
- Floating 3D sphere in corner
- No purpose
- Heavy performance cost
- Distraction
- **Why avoid:** Gimmicky, slow, doesn't serve the site

### ✗ Overused Startup Layout
- Hero → Features → Pricing → Social Proof → CTA
- Identical on 10,000 websites
- Boring rhythm
- Predictable
- **Why avoid:** Zero originality, forgotten immediately

### ✗ Repeated Section Rhythm
- Same spacing, same layouts
- No breathing room variation
- No pacing
- Monotonous scroll
- **Why avoid:** Boring, unmemorable, loses user attention

### ✗ Stock AI People
- Generic AI-generated headshots
- Obvious filters
- Uncanny valley
- Immediately dated
- **Why avoid:** Looks cheap, breaks brand trust

### ✗ Unreadable Experimental Type
- Tiny, hard-to-read fonts
- Weird weights that clash
- No hierarchy
- Looks like design exercise
- **Why avoid:** Inaccessible, frustrating, kills UX

### ✗ Excessive Glitch Effect
- Every transition glitches
- Overkill distortion
- Headache-inducing
- No restraint
- **Why avoid:** Annoying, inaccessible, hurts brand

### ✗ Motion That Doesn't Serve
- Animation for sake of animation
- Slows down interactions
- Distracting, not enhancing
- Ignores reduced motion preference
- **Why avoid:** Bad UX, accessibility risk, unprofessional

### ✗ Mobile-as-Afterthought Layout
- Desktop-first, mobile broken
- Text too small
- Buttons unclickable
- Horizontal scroll
- **Why avoid:** 50%+ of users have bad experience

## Pattern Variations to Watch

### Overly Trendy
- Whatever looks new might be gone next year
- Trend ≠ timeless
- Watch for: Arbitrary shapes, extreme colors, gimmicks

### Aesthetically Confused
- Mixing incompatible visual systems
- No reason for the mix
- Feels accidental
- Watch for: Dark + light, minimal + busy, clashing moods

### Performance-Killing
- Beautiful but slow
- Animations that tank frame rate
- Images not optimized
- Watch for: Heavy 3D, tons of auto-play videos, unoptimized assets

### Accessibility Sins
- Color alone to convey meaning
- No keyboard navigation
- No alt text
- Watch for: WCAG failures, screen reader nightmares

### Overuse of Signature Element
- One good idea repeated to death
- Dilutes its power
- Becomes gimmick
- Watch for: Overusing rare-use accents
