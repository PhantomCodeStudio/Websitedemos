# Quality Scorecard

**Status:** Pending website completion  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-29

---

## Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive QA framework for scoring the website design and implementation. A score of 85+ is required for project completion.

---

## Scoring Methodology

**Total possible: 100 points**  
**Passing score: 85+**  
**Distribution: 10 categories**

---

## Category 1: Originality (0-15 points)

**What it measures:** How original and distinctive the design is

### Scoring Guide

- **13-15:** Genuinely original, unexpected, memorable
- **10-12:** Original with some familiar elements
- **7-9:** Mostly original, some conventional choices
- **4-6:** Mix of original and generic
- **1-3:** Mostly copies or generic
- **0:** Completely generic

### Assessment Questions

1. Is this design distinctly different from competitor sites?
2. Does the design avoid clichéd SaaS patterns?
3. Are there unique visual choices or interactions?
4. Does the visual style feel fresh, not trendy?

### Your Score: /15

---

## Category 2: Client Fit (0-15 points)

**What it measures:** How well the design matches the client's brief and values

### Scoring Guide

- **13-15:** Perfect alignment with brief and client identity
- **10-12:** Strong alignment with minor disconnects
- **7-9:** Good alignment with some areas not matching
- **4-6:** Partial alignment, some significant gaps
- **1-3:** Weak alignment to brief
- **0:** Does not match brief

### Assessment Questions

1. Does the design reflect the client's industry/market?
2. Does it communicate the correct professional/creative split?
3. Are the tone and values expressed visually?
4. Would the client recognize this as theirs?

### Your Score: /15

---

## Category 3: Visual Hierarchy (0-15 points)

**What it measures:** Clarity of what's important; ease of visual scanning

### Scoring Guide

- **13-15:** Perfectly clear hierarchy; easy to scan; no confusion
- **10-12:** Clear hierarchy with minor inconsistencies
- **7-9:** Mostly clear, some sections are harder to parse
- **4-6:** Inconsistent hierarchy, some confusion
- **1-3:** Weak or confusing hierarchy
- **0:** No clear visual hierarchy

### Assessment Questions

1. Can users instantly identify the most important content?
2. Do headlines, subheadings, and body copy feel weighted correctly?
3. Are sections clearly separated or distinguished?
4. Does the visual weight guide the eye correctly?

### Your Score: /15

---

## Category 4: Typography (0-10 points)

**What it measures:** Font choices, sizes, spacing, and readability

### Scoring Guide

- **9-10:** Excellent typography; perfect pairing; excellent spacing; highly readable
- **7-8:** Good typography; appropriate choices; readable
- **5-6:** Acceptable typography; minor spacing or pairing issues
- **3-4:** Some typography problems; questionable readability
- **1-2:** Poor font choices or spacing
- **0:** Unreadable or terrible typography

### Assessment Questions

1. Are the typefaces well-paired and appropriate?
2. Are font sizes logical and readable at all breakpoints?
3. Is line height and letter spacing appropriate?
4. Are headlines and body text clearly differentiated?

### Your Score: /10

---

## Category 5: Motion Quality (0-10 points)

**What it measures:** Animation smoothness, purpose, and restraint

### Scoring Guide

- **9-10:** Smooth animations; each motion has purpose; feels premium
- **7-8:** Good motion; mostly purposeful; minor performance hiccups
- **5-6:** Decent motion; some unnecessary animations
- **3-4:** Motion has jank or purpose is unclear
- **1-2:** Jerky animations or too much arbitrary motion
- **0:** No motion or only broken motion

### Assessment Questions

1. Are all animations smooth (60fps)?
2. Does each motion serve a purpose (guidance, feedback, delight)?
3. Are transitions between states clear?
4. Is there motion overuse or underuse?

### Your Score: /10

---

## Category 6: Mobile Quality (0-10 points)

**What it measures:** Responsive design, touch interactions, mobile-specific optimization

### Scoring Guide

- **9-10:** Perfect mobile experience; optimized for touch; responsive
- **7-8:** Good mobile experience; minor responsive issues
- **5-6:** Mostly mobile-ready; some layout issues on smaller screens
- **3-4:** Mobile experience is poor; major layout problems
- **1-2:** Mobile barely usable
- **0:** Mobile experience is broken

### Assessment Questions

1. Does the layout work well on all screen sizes?
2. Are touch targets appropriately sized (44px minimum)?
3. Is mobile navigation functional?
4. Are images appropriately cropped for mobile?

### Your Score: /10

---

## Category 7: Image/Art Direction (0-10 points)

**What it measures:** Quality of visual assets, consistency, and strategic use

### Scoring Guide

- **9-10:** Excellent images; strategic use; visually cohesive
- **7-8:** Good images; mostly cohesive
- **5-6:** Acceptable images; some inconsistency
- **3-4:** Mediocre images; little cohesion
- **1-2:** Poor image quality or strategic use
- **0:** Stock images or inappropriate imagery

### Assessment Questions

1. Do images align with the visual direction?
2. Are images high quality and optimized?
3. Is there visual consistency across images?
4. Do images serve a strategic purpose?

### Your Score: /10

---

## Category 8: Performance (0-5 points)

**What it measures:** Page load speed, optimization, and technical performance

### Scoring Guide

- **5:** Lighthouse 90+; Fast load; smooth scrolling
- **4:** Lighthouse 80-89; Good performance
- **3:** Lighthouse 70-79; Acceptable performance
- **2:** Lighthouse 60-69; Some performance issues
- **1:** Lighthouse below 60; Multiple issues
- **0:** Poor performance; unacceptable load time

### Assessment Questions

1. Is the Lighthouse score 90+?
2. Are images properly optimized (WebP/AVIF)?
3. Is the code efficient (no render-blocking)?
4. Is the site fast on mobile connections?

### Your Score: /5

---

## Category 9: Accessibility (0-5 points)

**What it measures:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, inclusive design

### Scoring Guide

- **5:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliant; excellent accessibility
- **4:** Minor accessibility issues
- **3:** Some accessibility issues but mostly usable
- **2:** Significant accessibility barriers
- **1:** Major barriers to access
- **0:** Inaccessible to assistive technology users

### Assessment Questions

1. Is the site keyboard navigable?
2. Is color contrast sufficient (4.5:1)?
3. Are headings properly structured?
4. Do interactive elements have accessible labels?

### Your Score: /5

---

## Category 10: Conversion Clarity (0-5 points)

**What it measures:** Clear CTAs, conversion funnels, business goals

### Scoring Guide

- **5:** CTAs crystal clear; obvious conversion path
- **4:** CTAs clear; good conversion path
- **3:** CTAs present; conversion path adequate
- **2:** CTAs weak or unclear
- **1:** Poor conversion messaging
- **0:** No clear CTAs or conversion path

### Assessment Questions

1. Are primary CTAs clear and prominent?
2. Is the conversion goal obvious?
3. Are conversion paths logical and easy to follow?
4. Is contact information easily accessible?

### Your Score: /5
