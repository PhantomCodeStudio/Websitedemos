# /new-site

## Purpose
Start a new website project by gathering strategy, client info, constraints, and goals before any design direction is locked.

## What It Does
1. Asks about client type and business
2. Gathers project goals and constraints
3. Identifies target audience
4. Captures success metrics
5. Notes any existing branding/content
6. Creates initial DESIGN_BRIEF.md
7. Prepares for reference intake

## What It Reads
- (none yet - this is the starting command)

## What It Updates
- Creates/updates DESIGN_BRIEF.md

## Questions to Ask

1. **Who is the client?**
   - Business name/type
   - Industry
   - What they do
   - Current stage (startup, established, etc.)

2. **What is the website for?**
   - Primary purpose
   - Secondary goals
   - Target users
   - Main conversion action

3. **What constraints exist?**
   - Timeline
   - Budget reality
   - Technical limitations
   - Brand guidelines to follow
   - Content already available

4. **What should this feel like?**
   - 3 words describing desired impression
   - Competitor examples (what to avoid)
   - Personal reference aesthetics
   - Target maturity level

5. **Who is the audience?**
   - Age range
   - Experience level
   - Geographic location
   - Device preference
   - Pain point being solved

6. **Success metrics?**
   - What defines success
   - How to measure it
   - Timeline for results

## Do Not Do
- Do not lock visual direction yet
- Do not propose specific designs
- Do not make assumptions about style
- Do not create components yet

## Output
Detailed DESIGN_BRIEF.md ready for reference intake
